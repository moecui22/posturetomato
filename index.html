Common questions

Which headphones work?
AirPods (3rd generation), AirPods Pro (all generations), AirPods Max, and Beats Fit Pro. Older AirPods and AirPods (2nd generation) have no motion sensors and will not work for posture.
Can I use just one earbud?
Yes. The app detects which ear is in use, tells you, and draws only that bud on the tomato.
Do I need AirPods at all?
No. The timer, harvest crates, ketchup drawer and sounds all work without them. You only lose the posture half.
It says my AirPods are not connected, but they are.
Motion data only flows while the buds are in your ears. Take them out and the app sees the stream go silent within a few seconds. Put them back in and it reconnects on its own.
Does it tell me if my posture is good?
No, and deliberately so. Head orientation cannot see your spine. The app measures drift from the position you settled into at the start of the session — nothing more.
Why did my tomato turn into ketchup?
Ending a session early squashes the tomato into a ketchup sachet. It sits in the drawer for seven days, then expires.
How do I reset my baseline?
Recalibrate in Settings, or start a new session — each one begins with a five-second calibration.
